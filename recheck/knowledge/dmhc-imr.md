# Independent Medical Review (IMR) through the DMHC

Source note: summarized from search-result excerpts of DMHC pages; the page itself could not be fetched (HTTP 403).

- What: an independent review of a health plan's denial of care, available through the DMHC.
- Who can request: people enrolled in a DMHC-licensed plan. Not eligible: Medicare enrollees, Medi-Cal fee-for-service members, members of self-insured and self-funded plans, and workers' compensation disputes.
- Cost: free to the enrollee.
- Before filing: generally complete the plan's own grievance process first, or file if at least 30 days have passed since the grievance was filed with the plan. Urgent cases and experimental or investigational denials may go faster.
- Timeframe: IMR cases are generally decided within 45 days.
- Help: www.DMHC.ca.gov or 1-888-466-2219.
