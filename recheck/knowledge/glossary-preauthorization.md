# Preauthorization

A decision by your health insurer or plan that a care service, treatment plan, prescription drug, or medical equipment is medically necessary. It is also known as prior authorization, prior approval, or precertification. Your plan may require it for certain services before you receive them, except in emergencies. Preauthorization isn't a promise your health insurance or plan will cover the cost.
