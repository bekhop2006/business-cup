# Self-insured employer plans and state law (DOL Technical Release 2014-01)

Note: this is an EBSA technical release dated November 6, 2014. It is older guidance and its main topic is state regulation of stop-loss insurance.

## Background

Employers that self-insure their group health plans generally aren't subject to state health insurance laws. They often buy stop-loss insurance to protect against unpredictable or catastrophic claims. These policies pay out once claims exceed an attachment point, either for a single enrollee or for total plan claims.

## Legal backdrop

ERISA section 514(a) generally preempts state laws relating to employee benefit plans, and states can't treat plans as insurers to regulate them.

## Department's position

The Department of Labor believes states may regulate insurers that issue stop-loss policies, as long as the law regulates the insurance company and the business of insurance.
