# Grandfathered health plan

An individual health insurance policy bought on or before March 23, 2010, sold outside the Marketplace by insurers, agents, or brokers. It may lack some Affordable Care Act protections and can lose its grandfathered status if the insurer makes significant changes that cut benefits or raise costs. Group plans may add new members after that date.
