# Submit a provider or insurer complaint (No Surprises Help Desk)

Health insurers, health care providers, and health care facilities must follow federal rules that protect you from unexpected out-of-network bills, often called "surprise" bills. If you have a question about these rules, or you believe they aren't being followed, you can submit a question or complaint to the No Surprises Help Desk.

## What the Help Desk can do

- Review your complaint to determine whether your insurer, provider, or facility followed surprise billing rules.
- Investigate compliance with federal laws and policies under CMS's jurisdiction.
- Refer your complaint to another federal or state enforcement authority if necessary.

## Phone

Call 1-800-985-3059 to get help submitting a complaint or to learn your next steps. Help is available in English, Spanish, and over 350 other languages.

## What you need

Gather supporting documents before you start. More information generally means faster processing. You don't need every document, and you can add more later.

Provide if possible:

- Your medical bill
- A photo of your health insurance card, if you used insurance
- An explanation of benefits, if you received one

Include if available and relevant:

- Your good faith estimate
- A signed and dated notice and consent form
- Any correspondence with your provider
- Notice(s) of appeal decision
- Evidence of coverage, such as a summary plan or membership booklet

## What happens next

- Write down your confirmation number.
- If you provided an email address, you'll receive a message confirming your complaint. Keep it for your records.
- The Help Desk will review your complaint and documents. If it needs more information, it will contact you within 60 days using your preferred method.
- To check on your complaint, call 1-800-985-3059 and reference your confirmation number.
- To add documents later, return to your existing complaint and enter your confirmation number.

You can submit online ("Get Started" for a new complaint, or "Update an existing complaint") or by phone at 1-800-985-3059.
