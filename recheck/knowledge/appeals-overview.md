# Appeal an insurance company decision

If your insurer refuses to pay a claim or ends your coverage, you can appeal the decision and have a third party review it. Insurers must explain why they denied a claim or ended coverage, and they must tell you how to dispute that decision.

## Two types of health plan appeals

- Internal appeal: you ask your insurer to review its own decision. If your case is urgent, the insurer must speed up the process.
- External review: you take the appeal to an independent third party. The insurer no longer has the final say on whether a claim is paid.

## Other appeals

You can also appeal Marketplace decisions, such as being found ineligible to enroll in a Marketplace plan or being denied premium tax credits or other cost savings.
