# AB 72 (2016): surprise balance billing protections

Source note: summarized from search-result excerpts of DMHC guidance; the document itself could not be fetched (HTTP 403).

- What: California law (Health and Safety Code sections 1371.30, 1371.31 and 1371.9) limiting what patients owe when a non-contracted clinician treats them at a contracted (in-network) facility for non-emergency care.
- Patient cost: capped at the in-network cost-sharing amount.
- Plan payment: the plan reimburses the out-of-network provider the greater of the average contracted rate or 125 percent of the Medicare rate.
- Does not apply to: emergency services and care, dental providers, or Medi-Cal managed care plans. DMHC guidance also says it does not apply to self-insured plans.
- Narrow exception: for PPO-type products, an out-of-network provider may balance bill if the enrollee gave consent in compliance with section 1371.9(c).
- Disputes between providers and plans go to a DMHC independent dispute resolution process.
