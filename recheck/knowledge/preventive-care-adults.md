# Preventive care benefits for adults

Marketplace health plans and many other plans must cover the following adult preventive services. Coverage usually doesn't require a copayment or coinsurance, even if you haven't met your deductible, when you see an in-network provider. Coverage can vary, and $0 cost isn't guaranteed in every case.

- Abdominal aortic aneurysm screening: one-time, for men of specified ages who have ever smoked.
- Alcohol misuse screening and counseling.
- Aspirin use: to prevent cardiovascular disease and colorectal cancer, for adults 50 to 59 at high cardiovascular risk.
- Blood pressure screening.
- Cholesterol screening: for certain ages or higher-risk adults.
- Colorectal cancer screening: ages 45 to 75.
- Depression screening.
- Type 2 diabetes screening: ages 40 to 70 who are overweight or obese.
- Diet counseling: for adults at higher risk of chronic disease.
- Fall prevention: for adults 65 and older living in the community, using exercise or physical therapy and vitamin D.
- Hepatitis B screening: for people at high risk, including those from countries with high Hepatitis B prevalence and certain U.S.-born people who weren't vaccinated as infants and have a parent from a high-prevalence region.
- Hepatitis C screening: ages 18 to 79.
- HIV screening: ages 15 to 65, and other ages at increased risk.
- PrEP: HIV prevention medication for HIV-negative adults at high risk through sex or injection drug use.
- Immunizations: doses, recommended ages, and target populations vary. Vaccines covered include chickenpox, diphtheria, flu, hepatitis A and B, HPV, measles, meningococcal, mumps, whooping cough, pneumococcal, rubella, shingles, and tetanus.
- Lung cancer screening: ages 50 to 80 who are heavy smokers or quit within the past 15 years.
- Obesity screening and counseling.
- STI prevention counseling: for adults at higher risk.
- Statin preventive medication: ages 40 to 75 at high risk.
- Syphilis screening: for adults at higher risk.
- Tobacco use screening: for all adults, plus cessation interventions for tobacco users.
- Tuberculosis screening: for certain adults without symptoms who are at high risk.
