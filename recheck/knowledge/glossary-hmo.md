# Health Maintenance Organization (HMO)

A health insurance plan that generally covers only care from doctors employed by or contracted with the HMO, and typically doesn't cover out-of-network care except in emergencies. Some HMOs require you to live or work in their service area, and they emphasize coordinated care, prevention, and wellness.
