# High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)

A plan with a higher deductible than a traditional plan, usually with a lower monthly premium. You pay more of your own health care costs before the insurer starts covering its share. It can be paired with a health savings account (HSA), which lets you pay for certain medical expenses with tax-free savings, so it is also called an HSA-eligible plan.
