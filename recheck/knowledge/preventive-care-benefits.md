# Preventive health services

- Most health plans, including Marketplace plans, must cover a set of preventive services, such as screening tests, at no cost to you.
- Services are generally free when provided by an in-network medical provider.
- For certain services, such as immunizations and screening tests, you usually won't owe a copayment or coinsurance, even if you haven't met your deductible.
- Coverage varies, and "$0 cost isn't guaranteed in all cases."

## Preventive services by group

The page groups covered services into three sets, each with its own list: for all adults, for women, and for children.
