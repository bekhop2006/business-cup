# Out-of-pocket maximum/limit

The most you have to pay for covered services in a plan year. After you reach this amount through deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance for in-network care, your health plan covers 100% of covered benefits. Monthly premiums, services the plan doesn't cover, out-of-network care, and charges above the allowed amount don't count toward it. For Marketplace plans, the limit is $10,600 for an individual or $21,200 for a family in 2026, and $12,000 or $24,000 in 2027.
