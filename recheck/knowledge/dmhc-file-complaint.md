# Filing a complaint with the DMHC Help Center

Source note: summarized from search-result excerpts of DMHC pages; the page itself could not be fetched (HTTP 403).

- Who: people enrolled in a health plan licensed by the DMHC. If the plan is not DMHC-licensed, the Help Center refers the person to the right regulator.
- Step 1: file a grievance with the health plan for each issue and take part in the plan's grievance process for 30 days. The plan's member services number is on the membership card.
- Step 2: go to the DMHC if unhappy with the plan's answer, or if the plan has not decided within 30 days.
- Exceptions: urgent grievances can go straight to the DMHC. Help is also available right away when a request was denied as experimental or investigational.
- Timeframe: complaints are generally decided within 30 days of receipt.
- How: online at www.DMHC.ca.gov or by phone at 1-888-466-2219 (help is free).
