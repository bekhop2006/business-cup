# Preventive care benefits for women

Marketplace health plans and many other plans must cover the preventive services below for women without copayments or coinsurance, even before the deductible is met. This usually applies when you use an in-network provider. Coverage can vary, so $0 cost isn't guaranteed in every case.

## Services for pregnant women or those who may become pregnant

- Breastfeeding support, counseling, and supplies
- FDA-approved birth control, sterilization, and related education and counseling, as prescribed (excludes abortifacient drugs and doesn't apply to certain exempt religious employers)
- Folic acid supplements
- Gestational diabetes screening at 24 weeks or later, or for those at high risk
- Gonorrhea screening for higher-risk women
- Hepatitis B screening at the first prenatal visit
- Maternal depression screening at well-baby visits
- Preeclampsia prevention and screening for pregnant women with high blood pressure
- Rh incompatibility screening, with follow-up testing for higher-risk women
- Syphilis screening
- Expanded tobacco cessation counseling for pregnant tobacco users
- Urinary tract or other infection screening

## Other covered preventive services for women

- Bone density screening for women over 65, or younger postmenopausal women
- BRCA genetic counseling for women at higher risk
- Mammograms every 1-2 years for women 40 and older
- Breast cancer chemoprevention counseling for higher-risk women
- Cervical cancer screening (Pap test) for ages 21-65
- Chlamydia screening for younger and higher-risk women
- Diabetes screening for women with a history of gestational diabetes who aren't pregnant and haven't had type 2 diabetes diagnosed
- Domestic and interpersonal violence screening and counseling
- Gonorrhea screening for higher-risk women
- HIV screening and counseling for ages 15-65, and others at increased risk
- PrEP for HIV-negative women at high risk through sex or injection drug use
- Sexually transmitted infection counseling for sexually active women
- Tobacco use screening and intervention
- Urinary incontinence screening yearly
- Annual well-woman visits
