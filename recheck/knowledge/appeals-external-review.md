# External review

## Two steps

- Filing: you must submit a written request within 4 months of receiving your insurer's notice or final determination that your claim was denied.
- Decision: the external reviewer either upholds the insurer's decision or rules in your favor. Your insurer is required by law to accept the external reviewer's decision.

## Denials eligible for external review

- Denials involving medical judgment that you or your provider disagree with
- Determinations that a treatment is experimental or investigational
- Coverage cancellations based on the insurer's claim that you gave false or incomplete information when applying

## Your rights

- All states must offer an external review process that meets federal consumer protection standards.
- If your state has its own compliant process, insurers in your state follow it.
- If your state doesn't, HHS oversees an external review process for insurers in your state.
- Depending on your plan and location, insurers either participate in an HHS-administered process or contract with independent review organizations (IROs). Plans not in a state or HHS process must contract with an IRO.

## Finding your state's process

- Check your Explanation of Benefits or the final denial of your internal appeal for the contact information of the organization handling your review.
- HHS's Center for Consumer Information & Insurance Oversight maintains a state list.

## Timelines

- Standard review: decided no later than 45 days after the request is received.
- Expedited review: decided within 72 hours or less, depending on medical urgency.

## Filing with the HHS-administered process

- Submit online through MAXIMUS Federal Services' secure site.
- Call 1-888-866-6205 to request a form, then fax the completed request to 1-888-866-6190.
- Mail the form to MAXIMUS Federal Services, 3750 Monroe Avenue, Suite 705, Pittsford, NY 14534.
- Email ferp@maximus.com.

## Possible deadline extension (time-limited)

If your deadline fell between July 1 and August 3, 2026, and your plan uses the HHS-administered process, you may be eligible to file until October 2, 2026. This may apply to residents of Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Texas, Wisconsin, or U.S. territories other than Puerto Rico, and to people with state or local government plans in any state. Contact your plan to confirm. (This window had already ended as of 2026-11-15.)

## Representatives

You can appoint someone with knowledge of your medical condition, such as your doctor, to file on your behalf. The authorized representative form is on the MAXIMUS site.

## Cost

- Under the HHS-administered process, there is no charge.
- Under an IRO or state process, you may be charged, but no more than $25 per external review.

## Getting help

- Your state's Consumer Assistance Program or Department of Insurance can help with internal appeals and external reviews.
- General questions: 1-800-318-2596.
