# Which plans the DMHC regulates

Source note: summarized from search-result excerpts of DMHC pages; the page itself could not be fetched (HTTP 403).

- The DMHC handles complaints against plans licensed under the Knox-Keene Act: all HMOs and some PPO and EPO plans. Other PPO and EPO products sold by insurers licensed by the California Department of Insurance (CDI) are regulated by CDI.
- Covered California plans are overseen by both agencies, mostly the DMHC. A complaint sent to the wrong agency is forwarded.
- Self-funded employer plans are generally outside state oversight because federal law (ERISA) governs them. They are excluded from DMHC IMR.
- Exception: self-funded multiple employer welfare arrangements (MEWAs) are regulated by CDI.
