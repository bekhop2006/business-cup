Before you begin

A few things
to know.

What should I upload?

A readable PDF or photo of a medical bill or Explanation of Benefits (EOB). An EOB shows how your insurer processed a claim. Uploading both helps Recheck compare what you are being asked to pay.

Does a flag mean my bill is wrong?

A flag identifies something worth checking with your provider or insurer. Review the original document and confirm the details with them before deciding what to do.

Will Recheck contact my provider?

You choose what to send and when to call. Recheck prepares a draft letter and phone script for you to review and use yourself.
